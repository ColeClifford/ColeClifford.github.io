Cards by Cole

Local multiplayer card games for iPhone.

Overview

Cards by Cole brings classic card games to your iPhone. Gather your friends and play together in the same room — no internet connection, no accounts, no waiting. Just pick up and play.

Features

  • Euchre — 4-player trick-taking with teams, trump, and bowers. First to 10 wins.
  • War — 2-player head-to-head card battles. Simple enough for all ages.
  • Local Multiplayer — Connect instantly over WiFi or Bluetooth. No internet required.
  • Beautiful Themes — 6 card themes including Classic, Midnight, Royal, Neon, Vintage, and Arctic.
  • No Ads — Ever. No pay-to-win mechanics.
  • Built for iOS 26 — Designed with Apple's Liquid Glass design language.
