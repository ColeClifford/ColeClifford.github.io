Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Cards by Cole ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal data. Cards by Cole is designed with privacy as a core principle:

  • No accounts: The app does not require you to create an account or sign in.
  • No analytics: We do not use any analytics or tracking services.
  • No advertising: The app contains no ads and no ad-related tracking.
  • No server communication: The app does not send any data to external servers. All gameplay occurs locally between devices using Apple's MultipeerConnectivity framework.

Local Network Usage

Cards by Cole uses your local network (WiFi and Bluetooth) to discover and connect with nearby players for multiplayer gameplay. This communication happens directly between devices — no data passes through our servers or any third-party servers. Player display names are shared only with connected devices during a game session and are not stored or transmitted elsewhere.

In-App Purchases

Cards by Cole offers optional cosmetic card themes as in-app purchases. These transactions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not have access to your payment information. Purchase records are managed by Apple and linked to your Apple ID.

Data Storage

The app stores minimal preferences (such as your chosen card theme) locally on your device using standard iOS storage. This data is not transmitted or shared.

Children's Privacy

Cards by Cole does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The app is rated 4+ and contains no objectionable content.

Third-Party Services

Cards by Cole does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services. The only third-party service involved is Apple's App Store for processing in-app purchases.

Your Rights

Since we do not collect any personal data, there is no personal data to access, correct, or delete. If you have any concerns, please contact us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at cole@pickford.ai.
